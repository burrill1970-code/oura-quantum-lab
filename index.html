Oura Quantum Lab is a private personal research application that analyzes its owner’s Oura Ring data to establish physiological baselines and evaluate wellness claims.

The research dashboard runs locally on the owner’s Mac. This public website provides application information and policies; it does not host the dashboard or collect Oura health data.

Establish a baseline

Review historical sleep, nighttime heart rate variability, resting heart rate, readiness, temperature deviation, and activity trends.

Lock the reference

Document the baseline window and analysis choices before beginning an intervention.

Evaluate the evidence

Compare subsequent observations with the baseline, considering ordinary variation and confounding factors. Association alone does not establish causation.

Research sequence: establish and lock the physiological baseline before activating any Quantum Upgrade trial. This website does not activate a trial.

Clear limits

This observational N-of-1 tool is not a medical diagnostic service or evidence that a wellness product works. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Oura or Quantum Upgrade.
