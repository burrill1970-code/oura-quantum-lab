Privacy Policy

Personal research stays separate from this public information website.

Scope and current status

Oura Quantum Lab is a single-owner, local research application. At publication, its dashboard reports “Not connected” and zero imported records. This policy describes that current state and the data categories used by its Oura research workflow. It must be reviewed before any change to storage, access, sharing, or hosting.

Oura data access and purpose

Oura access requires the owner’s authorization. The dashboard is designed to retrieve historical sleep and daily sleep summaries, nighttime heart rate variability and resting heart rate, readiness, temperature deviation, and activity including steps. These measurements support physiological baselines, historical trends, and comparisons with dated research observations. Available data depends on Oura permissions and records. No Oura data is obtained through this public website.

Storage and protection

The research dashboard runs locally on the owner’s Mac and states that research data and tokens stay on that Mac and credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Health records, OAuth client secrets, access tokens, and refresh tokens are not included in this public website or its public GitHub repository. This website has no OAuth callback, health-data upload form, analytics script, advertising script, or application database.

Local files and any CSV exports must be protected by the owner’s Mac account permissions and device security. This policy does not claim that the research database or exports are independently encrypted, that backups are absent, or that deletion is automatic. Copies in backups or exports require separate handling.

Sharing and service providers

This public site does not sell health data or send it to advertisers. Oura processes the owner’s account and authorized API requests under its own policies. GitHub serves these public pages and may process ordinary connection information, including IP addresses and request logs, under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Visiting this site does not authorize access to an Oura account.

Disconnecting and deletion

The local dashboard provides a Disconnect Oura control. To stop access, revoke the application’s authorization in Oura as well. Revoking access does not, by itself, erase previously downloaded records. To erase research data, the owner must remove the local application’s research data files and any CSV exports, notes, and backup copies they wish to delete, and remove associated credentials from macOS Keychain. No verified one-click erasure function is represented here. This application cannot delete records held by Oura; manage those directly with Oura.

Retention and control

Research copies remain under the owner’s control until manually removed; no automatic retention period is promised. This application is for its owner, not public enrollment. For policy or deletion-process questions, contact the maintainer through the website repository. Do not post health records or credentials in public issues.
